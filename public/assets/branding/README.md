# Official Campaign Branding Assets Documentation

This directory contains the verified, authentic campaign emblems and identity marks for **NSS MJCET Nasha Mukti Abhiyaan**.

## Verified Assets Present

1. **`nss-logo.png`**:
   - **Entity**: National Service Scheme (NSS), Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India.
   - **Specification**: 1024x1024 RGBA transparent PNG containing the official Konark Sun Temple chariot wheel emblem in navy blue and red with standard bilingual inscription ("राष्ट्रीय सेवा योजना" / "NATIONAL SERVICE SCHEME").
   - **Usage**: Primary unit identity mark on the navigation header, campaign credentials badge, and institutional footer.

2. **`nmba-emblem.jpg`**:
   - **Entity**: Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan (NMBA), Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India.
   - **Specification**: 240x240 RGB circular emblem in forest deep green featuring the State Emblem of India (Lion Capital of Ashoka) and stylized bilingual foliage motif.
   - **Usage**: Official initiative co-sponsor badge and national campaign affiliation indicator.

3. **`my-bharat-logo.jpg`**:
   - **Entity**: MY Bharat (Mera Yuva Bharat), Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India.
   - **Specification**: 384x160 RGB banner mark with Indian tricolor ribbon motif and youth silhouette.
   - **Usage**: Youth volunteerism partner mark and national youth platform link.
